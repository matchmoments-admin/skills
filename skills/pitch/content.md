## Slide outline

1. **Cover** (navy, chromeless) — kicker "Australia's scam detection & intelligence platform"; display headline "Stop scams where they *start.*"; one standfirst sentence; foot `askarthur.au`.
2. **The problem** (cream) — "No one watches where scams are *born.*" Banks watch transactions, telcos block calls; the origination layer (look-alike domains, phishing sites, fake shops) goes unwatched. Gold stat: **A$2.18B** lost/year.
3. **What it is** (navy) — "One free checker. One compounding *intelligence engine.*" Two hairline columns: Consumer·Free (check anything, instant 3-tier verdict, every channel) / Intelligence·Compounding (20+ feeds, clone & brand monitoring, B2B API).
4. **The moat** (cream, statement) — "Every check makes the next one *smarter.*" The flywheel + uniquely-Australian real-time scam corpus = a data moat that widens with every report and feed.
5. **Proof** (navy) — "A production platform, *not a prototype.*" Four gold stat tiles: 20+ feeds · 6+ channels · <$0.001/check · May 2026 first bank alert.
6. **The clock / SPF** (cream) — "Built for the 1 July 2026 *deadline.*" 200+ banks/telcos/platforms must Prevent·Detect·Disrupt·Report; Ask Arthur maps to all four + free SPF self-assessment. Gold stat: **A$52.7M** max penalty/obligation.
7. **Work with us** (navy, chromeless) — "Let's stop scams at the *source.*" Em-dash CTA list: free checker · SPF self-assessment · B2B API pilot · add brand to clone-watch. Foot: askarthur.au · brendan@askarthur.au.
